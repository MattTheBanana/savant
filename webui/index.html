Editor controls
↑ / ↓Select the previous or next lyric line in the LRC grid.
← / →Nudge the selected line or seek during playback. Shift + arrows applies the configured multiplier or adjusts word edges.
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+YUndo / redo the last edit.
SpacePlay or pause. Ctrl + Space performs a block pause.
TTML word editing
Drag playheadSeek the audio to a precise time.
Left-drag waveformSeek by dragging the waveform.
Middle-dragPan within the line’s edge context.
Drag wordMove selected word(s); Ctrl-drag one selected word to move only it.
Shift-dragSnap the word or resized edge to the playhead when close, then to the adaptive time grid.
Drag empty trackBox-select words in one line; Shift-drag empty lane or waveform space to select across lines.
Ctrl+ASelect words on this line; Ctrl+Shift+A selects all word cues.
Shift+← / →Seek to the nearest word boundary. Plain arrows nudge the selected word or seek.
FAdd a timed word cue.
Ctrl + wheel zooms around the cursor. Fit Lines resets the timeline scale. Word dragging, box selection, and playhead controls are available in TTML mode.
