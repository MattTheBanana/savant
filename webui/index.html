Ctrl+Z undoes the last lyric edit and Ctrl+Y redoes it (Ctrl+Shift+Z also redoes). Keyboard behavior: Space starts/pauses playback when focus is outside an editable text cell; Ctrl+Space pauses and returns the playhead to the position it was at before playback started. Press F in TTML words view to add a timed word cue. When a lyric text field is focused, Space and all arrow keys remain normal text-editing keys. In TTML mode, drag the empty word track to box-select within the current line; Shift-drag empty lane/waveform space to box-select across lines. Drag any selected word to move the whole selection; Ctrl-drag a selected word moves only that word. Shift-drag snaps the selection edge first to the playhead when close, otherwise to adaptive timeline grid marks. Shift-drag a word's start/end handle to resize and snap that edge in the same way (10s, 5s, 1s, 0.5s, 0.1s, 0.05s, or 0.01s intervals selected automatically for the current zoom). In TTML words view, Ctrl+A selects words on the current line and Ctrl+Shift+A selects all word cues; in editable line text, Ctrl+A still selects only that text line. Click empty word-track space to deselect; dragging there box-selects. Drag the red playhead or left-drag the waveform to seek; the translucent markers show where the playhead time falls in overlapping line windows. Middle-drag pans only within each line's configured edge context. Ctrl+mouse wheel zooms at the cursor and redraws at most once per animation frame. While dragging a word near a lane edge, the view auto-scrolls at the configured speed. In TTML mode, Shift+←/→ moves the playhead to the nearest word boundary; plain ←/→ nudges a selected word or seeks in small increments if no word is selected.

Audio and lyric files are opened locally by your browser. This app does not upload them. Press Esc to leave a lyric text field and return to keyboard navigation.
